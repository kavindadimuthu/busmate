## What you are agreeing to

1. **What you contribute.** Stops, routes and timetables you have seen for yourself, with an honest
   account of when and how you observed them.
2. **Licence.** You give BusMate a permanent, free, worldwide licence to use, change and publish what
   you contribute, including with other data, and to pass it on to transport authorities.
3. **Credit.** Your contributions may be shown with the name on your account. You can ask for them to
   be shown without it.
4. **Honesty.** You will not submit anything you know to be false, and you will tell us if you work for,
   own or are paid by a bus operator.
5. **Review.** Everything you submit is checked by someone else before anyone sees it. BusMate may
   reject, change or remove any contribution, and may suspend you.
6. **Your data.** We keep what you tell us in this application only to run the contributor programme,
   and delete it with your account.
