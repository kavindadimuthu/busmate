OUR STORY

Built by riders, for riders

Public buses carry millions of people across Sri Lanka, yet finding the right bus, knowing when it will arrive, or booking a seat has too often meant guesswork and waiting.

BusMate was created to change that. We connect passengers, operators and route data in one place, so every trip starts with clear information and ends with confidence.

OUR MISSION

Reliable travel for everyone

Give every passenger accurate, real-time bus information so getting around is easier, safer and more predictable.

OUR VISION

Public transport people choose

A connected Sri Lanka where the bus is the first choice for every journey, backed by data passengers can trust.
